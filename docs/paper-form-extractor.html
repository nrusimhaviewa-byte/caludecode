Paper Form Digitizer — snap a photo, get structured fields.

Onboarding, KYC, and account-servicing paper forms still get keyed in by hand. This is a working, in-browser prototype: upload or photograph a paper form, OCR runs entirely client-side, likely fields get auto-matched, and a human confirms or corrects every value before it's exported — nothing is ever auto-submitted.

Problem

Ops teams still retype names, addresses, dates, and account numbers off scanned or photographed paper forms — slow, error-prone, and a bottleneck at onboarding.

Solution

Client-side OCR (Tesseract.js) reads the image in the browser, a lightweight matcher maps recognized lines to expected form fields, and every field ships with a confidence score.

Why it's safe to demo

Nothing is auto-approved. Low-confidence fields are flagged red, every value is editable, and the image never leaves the browser — no backend, no upload, no PII in transit.

Try it

Upload a photo or scan of any form (invoice, application, ID, intake sheet). Works best with printed text; handwriting recognition is limited — see the production note below.
