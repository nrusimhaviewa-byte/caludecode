Risks that could flip this next week

  • Crude & Hormuz tension: Brent spiked to $92/bbl mid-week after the US–Iran ceasefire collapsed, and eased back to $87.98/bbl by Friday — but the underlying tension hasn't resolved, so a fresh escalation could reignite the move (and re-pressure aviation/import-heavy names) fast.
  • Rupee near recent lows (₹95.40, easing with crude) — still raises import-cost and inflation risk if oil turns back up.
  • FII selling vs DII buying: FIIs pulled ₹1,601.65cr while DIIs added ₹17,316.34cr — domestic flows are the cushion right now, not foreign ones.
  • Market stays range-bound: consensus next-week range is 24,000–24,500; a break either side likely needs a fresh trigger (Fed commentary, crude move, or an earnings surprise).
