Risks that could flip this next week

  • Crude & Hormuz tension: Brent at $92/bbl after the US–Iran ceasefire collapsed — a real escalation risk that already broke a 4-session bounce once this week.
  • Rupee at a 6-week low (₹95.61) — raises import-cost and inflation risk, pressures rate-sensitive sectors.
  • FII selling vs DII buying: FIIs pulled ₹1,601.65cr while DIIs added ₹17,316.34cr — domestic flows are the cushion right now, not foreign ones.
  • Market stays range-bound: consensus next-week range is 24,000–24,500; a break either side likely needs a fresh trigger (Fed commentary, crude move, or an earnings surprise).
